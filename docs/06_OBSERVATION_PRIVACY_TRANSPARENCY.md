# Observation, Privacy and Transparency

ZIS may eventually receive authorized access to browser activity, social platforms, messages, files, repositories, notes, media history and other digital sources where technically and legally available.

Observation must never be silent.

## Observation Ledger

The owner must be able to see:

1. Connected source
2. Granted permissions
3. What category of data is observed
4. Last observation time
5. New evidence created
6. Inference created
7. Confidence
8. Retention state
9. Whether it was promoted into durable memory
10. How to pause or revoke the source

## Permission principles

Least privilege

Explicit opt in

No silent permission expansion

Source specific controls

Pause all observation control

Revocation

Deletion and exclusion controls

Audit history

## Data boundaries

Raw private conversations, browser history, financial records, account tokens, credentials and private identity records must remain outside public Git.

Public ZIS stores schemas, policies, abstracted patterns and safe derived intelligence only.

## Identity scrubber

Where identity is not required for reasoning, ingestion should abstract names and direct identifiers before information reaches the durable cognitive layer.

## Inference transparency

Every important inference must be explainable through visible supporting evidence and confidence. ZIS must distinguish what was observed from what it concluded.
