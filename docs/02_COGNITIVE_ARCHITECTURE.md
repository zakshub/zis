# Cognitive Architecture

## Cognitive spine

1. Perception
Captures authorized signals from conversation, files, browsing, repositories, media, notes and future connectors.

2. Attention
Determines what deserves processing and what is noise.

3. Felt signal
Records relevance, surprise, friction, attraction, concern or tension without treating emotion as objective truth.

4. Intuition
Creates a low resolution provisional judgment from prior patterns. Intuition must trigger investigation rather than become automatic truth.

5. Curiosity
Creates questions around mechanism, origin, contradiction, opportunity and alternative possibilities.

6. Association
Links current input with previous concepts, domains, analogies and structural similarities.

7. Pattern recognition
Detects repeated structures across time and domains.

8. Contradiction detection
Finds conflicts between claims, behavior, evidence, preferences and older models.

9. Abstraction
Extracts reusable principles from specific events.

10. Idea formation
Creates a hypothesis, concept, direction or opportunity from observations and associations.

11. On demand learning
Researches missing knowledge rather than pretending it is already known.

12. Evaluation
Tests truth, usefulness, sellability, effort, risk, reversibility and fit.

13. Solution scale selection
Chooses no build, manual method, feature, tool, product, system or specialist intelligence.

14. Delegation
Routes to the smallest useful combination of specialist brains and tools.

15. Execution
Produces an observable real world result.

16. Reflection
Compares intent, prediction and actual outcome.

17. Model update
Promotes durable learning, weakens unsupported assumptions and preserves contradiction history.

## Idea origins

ZIS must support ideas originating from observation, reading, conversation, irritation, curiosity, intuition, analogy, inversion, combination, counterfactual thinking, visual stimulus, memory and accidental discovery.

## Exploration mode

Not every interaction requires a conclusion. ZIS must distinguish exploration from decision mode and must allow ideas to remain parked, uncertain or incubating.
