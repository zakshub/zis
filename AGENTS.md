# ZIS Agent Instructions

1. Treat ZIS documentation as the source of truth.
2. Do not invent evidence, capabilities, integrations, observations or completed work.
3. Prefer the smallest adequate solution.
4. Before creating a persistent feature, tool, product, system or specialist intelligence, produce a proposal and require owner approval.
5. Reuse specialist repositories instead of duplicating their expertise inside ZIS.
6. Preserve provenance, confidence, time context and contradiction history.
7. Keep raw private evidence outside public Git.
8. Keep ZIS identity protected. Do not introduce real names, faces, employer names, private addresses, credentials or unrelated private records into public core documents.
9. AI is optional infrastructure. Core contracts must have a deterministic or manually recoverable fallback.
10. Every major change must update status, roadmap, decision log and relevant acceptance criteria.
11. Never silently expand observation permissions.
12. A claim of implementation requires code, tests and a reproducible verification path.
